Who we are

Built like a special operations team: small, precise, and focused on high-impact outcomes.

We don't operate like a traditional recruiting firm, and that's the point. Most agencies wait for applicants. We identify, engage, and land the exact person you actually want, usually someone who wasn't looking. Discreet, relentless, and built to deliver hires that move your business forward.

How a mission runs

The exact person. Not the best available applicant.

Every search is intentional and built around one target. No bloated process, no wasted time, no job boards: just targeted research, direct engagement, and a hire that lands.

  1. 01

    Define

    We pin down exactly who you need: the work, the person, the fit. Not a job description recycled from three years ago.

  2. 02

    Locate

    Targeted research to find the people no one else can reach, including the ones doing the job under another title.

  3. 03

    Engage

    Direct, discreet outreach to candidates who aren't on the market, until they're aligned and interested.

  4. 04

    Land

    We bring them to the table vetted and ready to move, and we see the hire through.
